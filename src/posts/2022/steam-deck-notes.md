While it doesn't have PS5-level hype &amp; popularity, it definitely is still one of the hottest topic in the circle. </p><p>That's why I am not gonna do a review, or tutorials, because not only are there <s>literally</s> virtually <a href="https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=steam+deck+review">thousands of videos on YouTube</a>, there is a very high chance that any future updates from Valve will completely change how it works, which currently happens about twice a week (if you are on the Beta channel of software updates) 😅</p><figure class="kg-card kg-embed-card"><blockquote class="tiktok-embed" cite="https://www.tiktok.com/@mrradtastic/video/7069987293209054507" data-video-id="7069987293209054507" style="max-width: 605px;min-width: 325px;"> <section> <a target="_blank" title="@mrradtastic" href="https://www.tiktok.com/@mrradtastic">@mrradtastic</a> <p>I literally died when I found out this happened <a title="rant" target="_blank" href="https://www.tiktok.com/tag/rant">#rant</a> <a title="literally" target="_blank" href="https://www.tiktok.com/tag/literally">#literally</a></p> <a target="_blank" title="♬ Literally just screaming - Mr Radtastic" href="https://www.tiktok.com/music/Literally-just-screaming-7069988145814719278">♬ Literally just screaming - Mr Radtastic</a> </section> </blockquote> <script async="" src="https://www.tiktok.com/embed.js"></script></figure><hr><h2 id="game-compatibility">Game compatibility</h2><p>In general, just go to <a href="https://www.protondb.com/">ProtonDB</a>, as well as the official verification badge to see if the game you want to play is compatible or not. Here I am just gonna list a few specific cases that was not super clear to me:<br></p><ul><li><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1250410/Microsoft_Flight_Simulator_Game_of_the_Year_Edition/"><em>Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020</em></a>: It is mark as no, not because it is too heavy of a game or something (that I know of), but because the part where it asks user to sign in to their Xbox account does not work. Either <a href="https://github.com/ValveSoftware/Proton/issues/4134#issuecomment-1106134197">the communication protocol or something is not supported</a>, or if you get the input screen showing, somehow there is no way to enter numbers for some god damn reasons, not even with Bluetooth keyboard 🤷‍♀️ 🤨 😑</li><li>A lot of mouse-first games that got verified: There are apps that have good full controller support, like <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/227300/Euro_Truck_Simulator_2/"><em>Euro Truck Simulator 2</em></a>; there are games that with full controller support that is just ok, like <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1455840/Dorfromantik/"><em>Dorfromantik</em></a> (unfortunately, using a 4 way joystick to navigate a hexagonal map is not as intuitive as I'd like); and there are games that works by basically just using the touchpad as mouse, like <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/240720/Getting_Over_It_with_Bennett_Foddy/"><em>Getting Over It with Bennett Foddy</em></a><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/240720/Getting_Over_It_with_Bennett_Foddy/">.</a> So your milage will definitely vary depends on what you want to play 🍀</li></ul><p>Generally speaking, Proton is both better and worse than I though it would be. I am amazed how many games just works, but somehow it is not ready yet for the 1 game I want to try the most on Steam Deck (no I am not doing Windows on Steam Deck. Just pick it if you don't want to bother with dealing with storage upgrade.</p><h2 id="other-issuesroom-for-improvements">Other issues/room for improvements</h2><p><strong>Hidden SSID Wi-Fi AP does not work</strong> (well technically it worked once and then never again), I haven't got around to figure out how to report bugs (that does not involve typing the whole ticket description using the trackpads)</p><p><strong>The 2 trackpad is definitely gonna take a lot of time to get used to</strong>, because a) they are tiny AF; b) there are 2 of them; c) they have absolutely terrible haptics (compare to Magic Trackpad), and d) they are emulating trackball and has the inertia and everything.</p><p>One thing to point out around <strong>all the noise complain</strong>, is that it also <strong>really depends on what games you play</strong>. Cause when I play driving simulator, the extra fan noise actually don't bother me at all. It's when I closed the game and realised how fucking loud it has been the entire time 🌪🌪🌪</p><p>Oh and <strong>the included wiping-cloth</strong>-looking thing looks awesome, but it <strong>is terrible as a wiping cloth</strong>, at least for the anti-glare glass. Just put a typical microfiber cloth that can soak up skin oil and stuff 🧼</p><hr><p>These are the list of very random, and definitely not planned out thoughts on my Steam Deck. I am very excited to see what else it can play next! And for those who are still waiting for your email from Valve, stay strong 💪 💪 💪 </p>
