<p><a href="__GHOST_URL__/purchases-2/#a-new-pc-8510">As stated about 4 months ago, I build my own PC</a>. Here are some of the use cases that I can confirm that desktop computer is good for</p><h2 id="1-gaming">1. Gaming</h2><p>Assuming you can get any reasonable GPU in reasonable price, it's really hard to beat gaming on desktop: it has better price-to-performance-ratio than laptop (again, assuming you can grab a GPU in normal price); it has the most open ecosystem (Steam, Epic Game Store, or install whatever game you have laying around); and said ecosystem allows a lot of pretty unique experiences: from playing MSFS in VR, to playing Among Us with friends while talking to your friends on Discord, and not having to subscribe to some monthly subscription that seems to go down once a month. It has become the biggest reason why I turn on the computer almost every day!</p><figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-width-wide"><img src="/assets/images/downloaded_images/2021/12/IMG_2649.jpg" class="kg-image" alt="Microsoft Flight Simulator on screen, the PC on the left, and a set of peripherals (Keyboard, Mouse, Xbox Wireless controller) in the front)" loading="lazy" width="2000" height="1500"  ></figure><h2 id="2-working-continuously-on-heavy-computation-tasks">2. Working continuously on heavy computation tasks</h2><p>Technically this is not experience of my current PC, but my previous work machine in the last company: about 2 years ago in another company, when they are swapping machines for our Android team, I requested an iMac, instead of MacBook Pro like everyone else, and I absolutely did not regret it at all! (except the part where I left like 4 months later, which makes me feel kinda like a jerk. Sorry Stefan 🙏)</p><figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-card-hascaption"><img src="/assets/images/downloaded_images/2021/12/image-1.png" class="kg-image" alt="A screenshot of a Tweet from Louis (@louis993546), which has a picture of an iMac in the boot screen, on an office desk. Most likely it will get its worth back in no time 😂</p><figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-card-hascaption"><img src="/assets/images/downloaded_images/2021/12/image-3.png" class="kg-image" alt="Israel Ferrer Camacho (@rallat)'s tweet, which states &quot;All mobile team at dropbox has 96GB Mac Pro&quot;" loading="lazy" width="1193" height="749"  ><figcaption>You don't have to go as crazy as Dropbox though (I think 🤔?)&nbsp;</figcaption></figure><hr><p>That's it. It's not a long list at all. That probably explains why most people I know are getting laptop nowadays. And I definitely miss the ability to, for example, type out this blog post anywhere in the world, which is why I also got myself a cheap(-ish) laptop for just that (<a href="__GHOST_URL__/samsung-galaxy-book-go-fiesco/">which unfortunately turned into a big mess</a>).</p><p>But at least at the time of the writing, I do not regret the decision of getting a desktop PC.
